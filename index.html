What is FitForge AI?

Most workout plans are one-size-fits-all. FitForge AI is different. It's a personalized workout planner and fitness tracker that uses artificial intelligence to design training programs around you — your body, your experience level, the equipment you actually have, and the time you can realistically commit.

Whether you're training for your first 5K, trying to build muscle, or just want to stay consistent, FitForge AI gives you a clear plan for every day and adapts it as you improve.
